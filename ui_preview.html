Tell me about a time you had to deal with a difficult stakeholder

Answer

Last year a product owner kept changing requirements two days before each release, and the team was reworking instead of shipping. I asked for fifteen minutes with her at the start of each sprint, and we agreed a freeze point after which only defects would be taken. I also published a one-page list of what was in flight so the change cost was visible to everyone. The next release went out on the agreed date, and the rework dropped from around a third of our capacity to under a tenth.
